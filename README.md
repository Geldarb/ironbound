# Ironbound (demo)

A small RuneScape-style idle game for phones and desktops. Plain ES modules + canvas, no build step, no runtime dependencies.

**Play loop:** create a character (name + look) → tap the ground to walk → tap a monster or resource node and the hero walks over, auto-attacks / auto-gathers and repeats on that target until you pick another (or HP gets low and you retreat) → smelt bars at the furnace, smith gear at the anvil, equip it, sell loot to the store, bank items.

## What is in the demo
- **Skills (RS XP curve, 1-99):** Attack, Strength, Defence, Hitpoints, Mining, Smithing, Woodcutting, Fishing, Cooking. Combat level uses the RS formula.
- **Combat:** RS accuracy and max-hit formulas (effective level + 64-based gear bonuses), 4 xp per damage split by stance (Accurate / Aggressive / Controlled / Defensive; Controlled shares xp three ways) plus 1.33 Hitpoints xp. The stance row is always visible and **follows your weapon** (each weapon has its own style names and damage types). Weapons have attack speed, per-type attack bonuses (stab/slash/crush) and a strength bonus; monsters have stab/slash/crush **defence values** (hill giants are weak to crush, wolves resist it), so the right weapon matters. Low HP (<40%) makes the hero retreat; dying sends you back to the start.
- **Areas:** Starter Meadow (chickens, cows, goblins, copper, tin, trees; bank chest, store, furnace, anvil, range, enchanting altar), Stony Quarry (wolves, the **Alpha wolf boss**, iron, **coal**, oak; combat level 10) and Windy Peaks (hill giants, **coal, mithril, adamantite, runite**; combat level 20). Walk to the signs or use the Map tab.
- **Weapons (11 kinds, RS-like):** dagger (fast, stab), sword (balanced), scimitar (fast, best slash accuracy), longsword, mace (crush), warhammer (slow, heavy crush), axe (also chops trees) / battleaxe, **2h sword** and **halberd** (two-handed: also take the shield slot), spear (reach, controlled) and halberd (long reach). Speeds 1.2-2.1 s (a tick is 0.3 s here). Weapon styles: dagger Stab/Slash/Block, scimitar Chop/Slash/Lunge/Block, mace Pound/Pummel/Spike/Block, spear Lunge/Swipe/Block, ...
- **Armour slots:** head, body, legs, weapon, shield, gloves, boots. Deterministic Bronze/Iron/Steel/Mithril/Adamant/Rune tables (requirements 1/1/5/20/30/40).
- **Smithing for every tier:** the furnace smelts bronze (copper+tin), iron (Smithing 15, 50% success), steel (iron + 2 coal), mithril (+4 coal), adamant (+6), rune (+8). The anvil has a tier selector (Bronze..Rune) and smiths all 11 weapon kinds, 6 armour kinds and pickaxes with RS-like bar counts (dagger 1, sword 1, scimitar 2, longsword 2, mace 1, axe 1, battleaxe 3, 2h 3, spear 1, halberd 2, warhammer 3). Stations unlock once you have walked up to them.
- **Enchanting:** **Enchant Points (EP)** come from level-ups (+1, +5 every 10th level), first visits to an area (+10), the Alpha wolf boss (+8) and a small trickle from kills. At the **Enchanting altar** (Meadow, or the Altar button in the Gear tab / item popup) spend EP to roll an enchant on a weapon, armour piece or tool: rarity Common/Uncommon/Rare/Epic/Legendary/Mythic (weights 60/25/10/4/0.9/0.1, **pity**: Rare+ guaranteed after 10 dry rolls, Epic+ after 20), 1-3 modifiers scaled by rarity from a pool of max hit, accuracy, attack speed, lifesteal, crit, slayer (+% vs beasts/humanoids/giants), burn procs, XP, drop chance, defence, regen and tool gather speed. Rerolls cost 10 EP x1.5 per roll on that item (armour 8, tools 5); you can **lock one modifier** for +50% and choose **Keep new / Keep old** after each reroll. Enchants live on the item instance (enchanted gear does not stack, stays on when equipped/banked) and count in combat, drops, XP and the offline simulation. Rarity colours show on bag borders, gear rows, inspect/compare and the altar.
- **Bag (28 slots) / bank / store:** stackables, bank chest in the Meadow, sell at 50% value, buy tools.
- **XP rate:** one constant, `XP_RATE` in `src/data.js` (now 3), multiplies every XP gain (combat, gathering, crafting). The level table is the real RuneScape one, so changing the rate never changes a stored level. Level 2 takes ~3 chicken kills in any stance; level 5 ~3-5 min, level 10 ~9-14 min of fighting.
- **Fishing + Cooking:** net spot (shrimp/anchovies) in the Meadow; rod + feather bait (trout/salmon), harpoon (tuna/swordfish) and lobster pot spots in the Quarry (level-gated). Cook at the Meadow range or either campfire (burn chance falls with level, range burns less; burnt food is left behind). Raw chicken/beef/wolf meat can be cooked too. Tools, bait and food-selling in the general store.
- **Eating:** tap the food chip (next to the HP bar) or a food item to eat (fixed heal, short delay between bites). **Auto-eat** (Settings: off / 30% / 50% / 70%) eats the best food in combat; with food in the bag the hero never retreats.
- **Offline progress:** the current target is saved every 3 s and on tab hide/close. After >60 s away the hero's fight or gathering is simulated at 50% pace, capped at 3 h (`OFFLINE` in `src/data.js`; code in `src/offline.js`), stopping early if HP/food/bait/tool would run out. A "Welcome back" popup lists time away, XP, level-ups, items, coins and food eaten; overflow goes to the bank.
- **Art + animation (all procedural canvas, no assets):** blocky RS-style hero with layered gear (helm, platebody, platelegs, kiteshield, gauntlets, boots) in per-tier palettes and a weapon sprite per kind (2h swords drawn large); idle breathing, walk cycle, per-weapon attack animations (stab thrust, slash arc with swoosh, crush overhead, 2h wide slash), hurt flinch, death fall; pickaxe swing with stone sparks, axe chops with wood chips, rod cast + bobber, net scoop with splash, cooking at a fire, smithing sparks, eating with crumbs, level-up fireworks, orange XP drops, RS-style hitsplats (red hit, blue miss, gold crit), green-over-red HP bars; blocky monsters with idle/attack animations and a death fall; rock -> dull rock and tree -> stump when depleted; pixel-tile grass, dirt path, fences, houses, lamps, pines, rubble, jagged parallax ranges, animated water ripples. Stone/gold UI skin with orange XP, parchment toasts and tab icons (still 44px targets, fits 360px).
- Saves to `localStorage` (single slot, versioned with migration; v3 adds EP/enchants/new slots, everyone gets a 20 EP welcome gift).

## Not yet (data structures are ready for them)
Co-op, trading, classes, ranged, magic, prayer, fletching/crafting/herblore, enchanted-item trading. Item/skill/recipe/area definitions are plain tables in `src/data.js`, so more tiers, skills and areas are data additions.

## Layout
`src/data.js` tables (weapon kinds, tiers, recipes) · `src/combat.js` pure formulas (styles, damage types, enchant mods) · `src/enchant.js` rarities, modifier pool, rolls, costs, pity · `src/game.js` model (inventory, crafting, area simulation) · `src/scene.js` canvas renderer + tap input · `src/art.js` procedural art · `src/ui.js` HUD, nav and bottom sheets · `src/save.js` · `server.js` static server with `/healthz`.

## Run / test
```
npm start            # node server.js, honours PORT, binds 0.0.0.0
npm test             # unit tests (XP, combat, tiers, recipes, gathering, saves)
node test/shots.mjs   # screenshot tour (tour | art | layout) -> /workspace/ib4-*.png
node test/e2e.mjs    # headless Chrome smoke test at 390x844 touch (needs puppeteer-core; PUPPETEER_PATH / CHROME_PATH)
```

## Deploy (Render web service)
Runtime Node, build `npm install`, start `node server.js`, health check `/healthz`. See `render.yaml`.
